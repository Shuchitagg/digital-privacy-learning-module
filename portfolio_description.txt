DIGITAL PRIVACY & SAFE ONLINE PRACTICES
Interactive Professional Learning Sample

Purpose:
A self-paced, platform-neutral learning module that demonstrates the ability to create accessible digital learning content and support learner engagement.

Key features:
• Three measurable learning objectives
• Plain-language instructional content
• Password-strength scenario
• Phishing warning-sign activity
• Drag-and-drop sorting exercise
• Five-question final knowledge check
• Automatic scoring and feedback
• Learner reflection and evaluation questions
• Responsive and keyboard-friendly design
• No collection or transmission of personal information

Resume project bullet:
Created a responsive interactive learning module with scenario-based instruction, knowledge checks, drag-and-drop practice, automatic scoring, learner feedback, and accessible navigation to demonstrate digital-learning content development and platform support capabilities.

Portfolio caption:
Digital Privacy & Safe Online Practices is an original, self-contained learning module designed to demonstrate professional-learning support, interactive content development, user-centred communication, and quality assurance.
